Method. Microsoft Clarity behavioural data for project tri2reya44, 1 June – 31 July 2026, filtered to paid sources (Meta, Google, TikTok, Bing). Aggregate metrics cover every session in the period. Session recordings are a curated sample of 915 sessions selected for rage clicks, dead clicks, quickbacks and best-in-class engagement — they are evidence, not a representative sample. Health score is a composite of scroll depth (30), engagement quality (25, penalising implausibly long dwell), friction from rage/dead/quickback clicks (25) and page speed (20). Suspected invalid traffic flags rows with a rage rate above 25%, scroll under 32% and dwell over 150 seconds — a combination that does not occur in genuine human browsing. No cost or conversion data is joined; this measures behaviour only.
