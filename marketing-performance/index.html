Definitions, scope, formulas, and known limitations
Scope. Actual paid-marketing results queried live from the Daily MW Report Power BI semantic model for 1–22 September 2026. PPC groups Google Search and Bing Search. Social is Facebook and Instagram. Demo Competition, IB campaigns, Google Display, and Social Boosting Posts are excluded, matching the pacing tracker's rules.
Plan values. Spend, CPL and leads per market and channel come from the 10 September 2026 marketing budget ($1.2M: $700k PPC, $500k Social), pro-rated to day 22 of 30 for month-to-date pacing. No other metric in this report has a plan, so accounts, funded accounts, deposits, withdrawals, NMI and ROI are shown as actual only.
Cost formulas. CPC = spend ÷ clicks. CPL = spend ÷ leads. CPA = spend ÷ live accounts. CPFA = spend ÷ funded accounts.
Conversion formulas. Lead-to-account = live accounts ÷ leads × 100. Account-to-funded = funded accounts ÷ live accounts × 100.
Cash formulas. NMI = total deposits + total withdrawals (withdrawals are negative). ROI = NMI ÷ spend.
A known limitation. First-time-deposit dollars, re-deposits and average account size do not reconcile per market or per asset type in this Power BI model — the FTD measure's campaign attribution only ties out at the channel level. Those three figures are shown only in the PPC/Social mix table and funnel, not in the Markets or Assets tables.
Sales desks. Desk actuals are estimated by applying each desk's fixed share of each country (from the budget workbook's share matrix) to that country's live Power BI leads. This differs from the previous manual desk report and will match it only if desk shares are unchanged.
Asset types. Read directly from each campaign's Category field in Power BI (the same field the naming convention encodes as a subcategory). Brand and Competitor search are excluded from this view and tracked instead in the Markets table's PPC split.
Recommendations. Generated from four rules on every refresh, not written by hand: scale the cheapest asset type with real lead volume, flag any asset type spending with zero leads, flag spend pacing under 70% or over 130% of month-to-date target, and flag whichever of PPC/Social is materially cheaper per lead here.
Source. Live DAX query against the Daily MW Report semantic model, plus the 10 September 2026 marketing budget workbook for plan values and desk shares, plus the team's PPC/Paid Social change log for the summary section.
